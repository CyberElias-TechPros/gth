The building, before the name changed

Real photographs of 273/274 Aba Road — from its Claridon Hotels & Resorts years.

Why these photos exist

The only pictures of this address we could actually verify

Grand Tokyu Hotel’s own website is currently offline and it publishes no photo library, and its Tripadvisor listing has no photos or reviews yet. But the address has a history: 273/274 Aba Road traded for years as Claridon Hotels & Resorts, and a set of its photographs survives on Tripadvisor and the OTAs.

They are published here — plainly labelled — because they are the only genuine photographs of the building we could find. They show the property as it was, not necessarily as it is today: Grand Tokyu has since refurbished and rebranded it.

Read this before you book on the strength of these pictures. These are archive photographs of the building from the Claridon era. Room finishes, furniture, the restaurant and the pool area may all have changed since. For how the hotel presents itself now, see the rooms and gallery pages — and note that the marketing imagery there is explicitly illustrative.

What we did not use

Photographs of Tokyu Grand Hotel off 22 Stadium Road — a separate hotel about 4 km away — or of any other Port Harcourt property whose listings happened to appear in the same searches. Where a photo could not be tied to 273/274 Aba Road, it was discarded.

Have current photographs?

Send them to the hotel, or drop them into images/official/ in this project and follow MEDIA.md. They can replace both the archive set and the illustrative imagery in a single rebuild.
