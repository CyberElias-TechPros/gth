About the hotel

Hospitality meets heart on Aba Road.

Our story

A Port Harcourt address, done properly

Grand Tokyu Hotel is a hotel for people who want the city within reach but the noise kept at the door. It sits on Aba Road, one of Port Harcourt’s main arteries, close enough to GRA Junction and Mile 1 Park to make the day easy, and far enough from the traffic to make the evening quiet.

Inside, the hotel works the way a good hotel should. Reception sets the tone the moment you arrive. The rooms are furnished for real life — plush bedding, soft linen, spacious wardrobes, a proper desk, air conditioning and a flat-screen television. The bathrooms are sleek, with hot water, warm lighting and premium toiletries.

Then there is the rest of it: a restaurant cooking Nigerian and continental food, a bar and lounge, a courtyard pool for the slow hours, and a front desk that stays awake so you do not have to worry about the time.
