Inside your room: what is already waiting

February 2026 · 3 min read

Every room at Grand Tokyu is built around the same idea: comfort should be effortless. Here is exactly what that means, category by category, so you can book the room that matches the trip.

Standard Room — ₦40,000

Sleeps two, with a king bed and a 40-inch Samsung® LED television. Non-smoking, free Wi-Fi and a USB charging station beside the bed. The right choice for a solo business trip or a couple’s weekend.

Classic Room — ₦50,000

Sleeps three and adds a proper work desk to the same 40-inch LED television, free Wi-Fi, non-smoking policy and USB charging. If you plan to answer email at midnight, take this one.

Standard Suite Room — ₦60,000

Sleeps four with a king bed and a city view — the hotel recommends it for families, and the extra space is what makes that work.

Classic Suite Room — ₦85,000

Sleeps five with a king bed, a sea view, a mini bar, a work desk, a 40-inch LED television, free Wi-Fi and USB charging. The signature suite, and the one to book for a celebration.

The Apartment — ₦300,000

A different proposition entirely: two separate bedrooms, a furnished living room with a powerful standing air conditioner, a fully equipped kitchen with a modern refrigerator and a washing machine, and a 75-inch LED television. Sleeps up to ten. Ideal for a family or a group staying long enough to want a home rather than a hotel room.

In every room

  • Air conditioning and a flat-screen television
  • Free high-speed Wi-Fi throughout the hotel
  • Rainfall shower, premium toiletries and warm lighting
  • Soft linen, spacious wardrobes and daily housekeeping
  • A 24-hour front desk and room service
