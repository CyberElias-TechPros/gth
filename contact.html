Getting here

Five minutes from GRA Junction

By car, the hotel is about five minutes from GRA Junction and ten minutes from Mile 1 Park. Port Harcourt International Airport is roughly 35 minutes away by taxi — ask the front desk to arrange a pick-up and we will meet you at arrivals.
