The Hall

Room for the whole guest list

Full-height, air-conditioned and deliberately flexible, The Hall is the hotel’s largest space. Four standing air conditioners keep it cool, chairs are included, and the Wi-Fi is free — so the only thing you need to think about is the programme.

Weddings, conferences, seminars, board meetings, birthdays and launches have all found a home here. The hotel’s kitchen caters from the same building, which means hot food arrives hot.

  • Max guests200 adults
  • Ratefrom ₦350,000 per night
  • Included4 standing A/Cs, free chairs, free Wi-Fi
  • Cateringavailable from the hotel kitchen
