Dining

Local and continental cooking, a rooftop bar and a poolside that is hard to leave.

The restaurant

Food that tastes like somebody thought about it

Cooking here moves between the Nigerian classics you grew up with and the continental dishes you crave after a long flight — jollof and grilled fish one night, a rested fillet the next. The kitchen keeps the menu generous, the portions honest and the seasoning confident.

Breakfast is served in the dining room, lunch runs into the afternoon, and dinner is available late. Room service brings the same food upstairs, so the only decision left is whether to dress for dinner.

Private dining

Birthdays, boardrooms and long lunches

Small celebrations can be hosted in the restaurant or up on the rooftop, and larger gatherings move to The Hall, which takes up to 200 guests with four standing air conditioners and free Wi-Fi.

Tell the team the date, the number of guests and the mood you are after, and they will build the menu around it.
