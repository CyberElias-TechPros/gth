The Grand Tokyu promise

A five-star Michelin experience

That is the standard the hotel sets itself: rooms that feel finished, a kitchen that cooks with intent, and service that notices before you ask — the promise the team is measured against every day.

What that looks like in practice

  • Rooms turned daily, linen changed and aired
  • A kitchen that cooks Nigerian and continental menus to order
  • Two reservation lines answered around the clock
  • Events handled end to end — layout, catering, timing
  • Airport pick-up and car hire arranged on request
